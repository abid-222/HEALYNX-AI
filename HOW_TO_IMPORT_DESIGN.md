## 1. 🚀 How to Open in **Figma** (Recommended)

1. Open your browser or Figma desktop app ([figma.com](https://figma.com)).
2. Open any new or existing Figma file.
3. Simply **Drag & Drop** [`HEALYNX_AI_FULL_DESIGN_ARTBOARDS.svg`](./HEALYNX_AI_FULL_DESIGN_ARTBOARDS.svg) from your Windows File Explorer directly onto the Figma canvas.
4. **All 6 Artboards** will appear in full vector fidelity:
   - ✅ Every card, button, and frame is an editable vector layer.
   - ✅ Every text label is editable.
   - ✅ Gradients, glows, badges, and charts are fully intact.
5. If you want individual frames, press `Ctrl + Shift + G` to ungroup or select any artboard frame.

---

## 2. 🎨 How to Open in **Canva**

1. Go to [canva.com](https://canva.com).
2. Click **Create a design** (top right) ➔ **Import file**.
3. Select [`HEALYNX_AI_FULL_DESIGN_ARTBOARDS.svg`](./HEALYNX_AI_FULL_DESIGN_ARTBOARDS.svg).
4. Canva will parse all vector paths, text blocks, and color palettes into editable Canva elements.

---

## 3. 🎯 How to Open in **Adobe XD**

1. Launch Adobe XD.
2. Go to **File ➔ Open** or **File ➔ Import** (`Ctrl + Shift + I`).
3. Select [`HEALYNX_AI_FULL_DESIGN_ARTBOARDS.svg`](./HEALYNX_AI_FULL_DESIGN_ARTBOARDS.svg).
4. All artboards will load with their original dimensions, vector curves, and dark-theme gradients.
